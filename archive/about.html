About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate from the University of Memphis. I completed my Bachelor of Science with magna cum laude honors and gained practical experience through software internships and team projects.

My interest in Computer Science started in middle school, when I began exploring how software and websites worked. That curiosity grew into an interest in building useful applications, improving interfaces, working with databases, and solving problems through code.

My experience includes front-end development, Flask and Python routing, MySQL database work, UI/UX prototyping, and collaborative application development. I enjoy learning new technologies and turning ideas into maintainable, user-friendly software.
